# 🌟 Tribute Page – Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

A responsive and visually appealing tribute webpage dedicated to **Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam**, one of India's most respected scientists, educators, and the 11th President of India.

This project was completed as **Level 2 – Task 2** during my internship at **Oasis Infobyte**.

- **Tribute To:** Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project is a tribute webpage created to honor the life, achievements, and legacy of **Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam**, popularly known as the **"Missile Man of India"** and the **"People's President."**

The webpage presents information about his inspiring journey, important milestones, contributions, and a memorable quote.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎨 Clean and visually appealing design
- 📱 Responsive layout
- 👤 Dedicated hero section
- 📖 Biography and introduction
- 🖼️ Image section
- 🗓️ Interactive-style timeline of important milestones
- 💬 Inspirational quote section
- 🦶 Simple and elegant footer
- ✨ Custom typography using Google Fonts

---

## 🖥️ Website Sections

### 🏠 Header

The header introduces Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam with the title:

> **The People's President & Missile Man of India**

---

### 👨‍🔬 Remembering a Visionary

This section highlights:

- His journey from Rameswaram to becoming India's President
- His contributions to India's scientific development
- His role in space and missile programs
- His dedication to education and young people
- His values of humility, leadership, and lifelong learning

---

### 🗓️ Journey Through Time

The webpage includes a timeline highlighting important milestones:

| Year | Achievement |
|------|-------------|
| 1931 | Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu |
| 1960 | Began his career as a scientist |
| 1980 | Led the successful SLV-III project |
| 1998 | Played a significant role in Pokhran-II |
| 2002 | Became the 11th President of India |
| 2015 | Left behind an inspiring legacy |

---

### 💬 Inspirational Quote

The tribute page features one of Dr. Kalam's inspirational messages:

> "Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."
